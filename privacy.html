Inkswing Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-02

Inkswing ("the game") is made by Fhalko Labs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services built into it collect, why, and the choices you have. If you have a question, write to inkswing.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. Your progress, settings, the name you give your seal and your paintings are stored on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob and can sell items through Google Play. These services, and Google's analytics and crash reporting, receive some device and usage data as described below.
  • You can turn off usage data and crash reports in Settings, change your ad privacy choices, and reset your advertising ID in Android's settings.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, the name you type for your seal, and the paintings you make are saved on your device. We do not receive them. If Android backup is on, Android may back this data up to your Google account so it survives a new phone; Google handles that backup under its own terms. Uninstalling the game deletes this data from the device.

Information collected by services in the game

Advertising (Google AdMob). To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob automatically collects your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, diagnostic information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Usage data and crash reports (Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics). If enabled, the game reports which screens you open, how runs go (for example chapter, length and result), purchases and ad views, together with an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version and approximate location (country). When the game crashes, it sends a crash report with technical details of the error. We use this to balance the game and fix bugs. You can turn this off in Settings → Share usage data and crash reports. Learn more: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat). Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. To deliver and restore what you buy, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives your purchase history and an anonymous identifier created for the game. Learn more: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games). If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your scores and achievements are sent to Google Play Games under your Play Games profile and shown to other players as Google Play Games allows. Learn more: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Reminders. If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device. No information is sent anywhere to do this.

Sharing paintings. A painting leaves your device only when you share it yourself through Android's share sheet.

How information is used

To run the game and deliver purchases; to show and measure ads; to understand how the game is played so we can improve and balance it; to find and fix crashes; and to prevent fraud. We do not sell personal information.

Security and retention

Information sent by the services above travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Each service keeps data under its own retention policy: Firebase Analytics data is kept for 14 months, Crashlytics crash reports for 90 days. Data on your device stays until you delete the game.

Your choices and rights

  • Settings → Share usage data and crash reports turns analytics and crash reporting off.
  • Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you change consent for personalised ads (where shown).
  • Android Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • To ask about or delete data the services hold for your device, write to inkswing.support@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, include the approximate date you played so we can help.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Write to us to use them.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date, and significant changes will be noted in the game's release notes.
